Every coin starts in the lobby. Ride it to the top.

Call the elevator for free, with no dev wallet. While the doors are open, anyone can step in, and when the car reaches the top floor the coin launches with everyone aboard buying at one price. HOW IT WORKSLobby, floors, penthouse.

01_lobby.txt×
01

Call the elevator

Sign in with Google, email or X, pick a ticker, a name and a ride of 5 to 30 minutes, and share it. The site pays the launch fee and there is no dev wallet. Anyone can board with up to 2 SOL; at the top floor one transaction launches the coin and buys it for everyone aboard at one price. A ticker, name, X profile or website gets one ride a day.
02_floors.txt×
02

Climb the floors

Trade with SOL on a bonding curve (1% curve fee, 0.25% app fee, half to the referrer). Every coin has a floor: one floor for every $2K of market cap. Fill the curve and the coin graduates to a locked pool and gets the keys to the penthouse.
03_lights_out.txt×
03

Lights out

Every trade pays rent. 40% buys back the coin and burns it, 35% burns $PETRONAS, and 25% goes to the building fund. Each coin switches its lights out at a random moment 15–25 minutes after the last time, once it holds at least 0.01 SOL. Every burn has a receipt.
